The installer works on any Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 server, whichever provider
you choose. What usually decides the choice from Nigeria is not price but
**whether your card is accepted**.

| Provider | Server to choose | Rough cost | Pays with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hetzner Cloud** | CX22, 2 vCPU / 4 GB | ~€4–5 a month | Card **or PayPal** |
| **DigitalOcean** | Basic Droplet, 2 vCPU / 4 GB | ~$24 a month | Card **or PayPal** |
| **Contabo** | Cloud VPS 10, 4 vCPU / 8 GB | ~€5–6 a month | Card **or PayPal** |
| **Frappe Cloud** | Private bench | Check their pricing | Card |
| Google Cloud, AWS, Azure | 2 vCPU / 4 GB | Trial credit | Card only |
| Oracle Always Free | Ampere A1 | Free | Card, for verification |

Prices change; treat them as a guide.

## Recommendation

**Hetzner** if your account is approved: cheapest reliable option, and it
takes PayPal. Otherwise **DigitalOcean**, which also takes PayPal and has the
simplest dashboard.

If cards keep being declined, a **USD virtual card** from a Nigerian fintech
is what people most commonly use. PayPal avoids the question entirely.

<Note>
Oracle's Always Free tier is genuinely free and generous, but its sign-up
frequently rejects Nigerian cards. Do not build a plan around getting through
it.
</Note>

**Students:** the GitHub Student Developer Pack includes DigitalOcean credit,
and Azure for Students needs no card at all.
