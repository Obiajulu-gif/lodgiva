## Room type or room?

Guests book a **room type** at a price. Staff assign a **room**.

```text
Room type: Deluxe — ₦85,000 a night
  ├── Room 101
  ├── Room 102
  └── Room 103
```

This separation is what lets you move a guest from 101 to 102 without
touching their booking or their bill.
