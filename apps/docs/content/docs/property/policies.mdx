---
title: "Policies"
description: "Check-in and check-out times, deposits, and what happens when a guest cancels or fails to arrive."
---

Policies are set once and then shown to guests on your booking page and
applied automatically.

## Check-in and check-out times

Set under **Settings → Property**. The defaults are check-in from 14:00 and
check-out by 11:00. They appear on your booking page and on confirmations.

Early check-in and late check-out can be charged as their own line on the
bill; both exist as charge types.

## Deposits

A refundable deposit can be requested at booking, shown separately from the
room price so guests are not surprised:

```text
₦96,750 total · 2 nights, taxes in
Refundable deposit ₦5,000 due separately
```

## Cancellation

A cancellation policy sets how late a guest may cancel free of charge, and
what is charged after that: the first night, or the whole stay.

When a booking is cancelled after the free window, Lodgiva posts the fee to
the bill as its own line, with the reason attached.

## No-shows

A guest who never arrives is flagged by the [night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit)
as `No Show`, and the policy fee is charged if one is set. This is why running
the audit every night matters: it is what turns an empty room into a recorded
decision.
