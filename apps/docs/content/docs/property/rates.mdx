## The base price

Every room type has a base price per night, set when you create it. That price
applies to every night unless something overrides it.

Prices are **before VAT**. A ₦85,000 Deluxe shows the guest ₦85,000 plus
₦6,375 VAT.

## Seasons

A season changes the price between two dates: Christmas, Easter, a conference
week, or simply weekends.

<Steps>

<Step>
Go to **Settings → Seasons** and select **New**.
</Step>

<Step>
Choose the **room type**, the **start and end dates**, and the adjustment:
either a fixed price for those nights, or a percentage change.
</Step>

<Step>
Save. Quotes and your booking page use it immediately for nights in range.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
The setup wizard can create a weekend season for you: give a room type a
weekend price and Lodgiva creates a Friday and Saturday season covering the
next two years.
</Note>

## Rate plans

A rate plan is a named way of selling the same room, such as "Room only" or
"Bed and breakfast". Meal plans attach a per-adult price to a plan, so
breakfast lands on the bill automatically.

## Overbooking

Lodgiva refuses to sell more rooms than exist:

```text
Lodgiva Demo Hotel-DLX is sold out for 2026-09-21:
2 of 2 rooms sold and the overbooking allowance (0.0%) is used up.
```

If you deliberately oversell, raise the allowance in **Settings → Guardrails**.
Leave it at zero unless you have a walk agreement with another hotel.

## Changing a price

Price changes apply to **new** bookings. Existing bookings keep the price they
were sold at, and invoices already issued never move.
