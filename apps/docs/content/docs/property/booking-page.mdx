---

Lodgiva gives every property a public booking page at **`/book`**, for example
`https://hotel.example.com/book`. Guests see live availability and pay you
directly, with no channel commission.

![The guest booking page: cover photo, nightly prices in naira and room cards](/images/docs/booking-engine.jpg)

## What guests see

- Your cover photo, hotel name and city.
- A date and guest picker, with nights calculated.
- Each available room type, its photos, and the **total price for the stay
  with taxes included**, in naira.
- Your check-in and check-out times, deposit and cancellation policy.

Room types with no availability for the chosen dates are hidden rather than
shown as unavailable, so the page stays useful.

## Add photos and descriptions

<Steps>

<Step>
Go to **Settings → Booking page**.
</Step>

<Step>
Upload a **cover photo** for the hotel, then photos for each room type. Add a
one-line description of each room type: what the bed is, what the view is,
what is included.
</Step>

<Step>
Save, then open `/book` and check it as a guest would.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
Photograph rooms in daylight with the curtains open and the bed made. The
booking page is often the first thing a guest sees.
</Tip>

## Sharing the link

Put the `/book` link in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp Business profile and
Google Business listing. Every booking through it costs no commission.

## Prices shown

Prices come from the room type and any [season](/docs/property/rates) covering
those nights, with VAT included in the displayed total. What the guest sees is
what they pay.
