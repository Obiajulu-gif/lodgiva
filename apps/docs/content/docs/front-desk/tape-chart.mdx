---

The tape chart is the grid every hotel recognises: rooms down the side, nights
across the top, each booking a bar.

![The tape chart with rooms down the side and nights across the top](/images/docs/tape-chart.jpg)

## Reading it

- A **bar** is one reservation, running from arrival to departure.
- A **gap** between bars is a sellable night.
- Colour shows status: confirmed, checked in, and rooms that cannot be sold.
- Rooms that are **Out of Order** are marked and cannot take a booking.

## What you can do from it

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Create a booking | Select the empty nights in a room |
| Open a reservation | Select its bar |
| Move a guest | Drag the bar to another room of the same type |
| Extend a stay | Drag the right edge of the bar |

<Warning>
Dragging a bar changes a real booking. Lodgiva checks availability first and
refuses a move that would double-book a room, but it will not ask twice, so
check the guest's name before you drag.
</Warning>

## Spotting an orphan night

A single free night between two bookings is hard to sell at full price. The
tape chart makes those gaps obvious; a small discount or a room move can join
two bookings and free a sellable stretch.
