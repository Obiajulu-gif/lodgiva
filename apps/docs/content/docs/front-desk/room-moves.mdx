---
title: "Room moves"
description: "Move a guest to another room mid-stay without disturbing their bill."
---

Guests move rooms for good reasons: a faulty air conditioner, a noisy
corridor, an upgrade. Lodgiva moves the guest, and the bill follows.

## Move a guest

<Steps>

<Step>
Open the reservation, from the in-house list or the tape chart.
</Step>

<Step>
Select **Move room** and choose the new room. Only rooms that are free for the
remaining nights are offered.
</Step>

<Step>
Confirm. The old room becomes vacant and dirty; the new room becomes occupied.
The folio, charges and payments stay with the guest.
</Step>

</Steps>

You can also drag the booking to another row on the
[tape chart](/docs/front-desk/tape-chart).

## Moving to a different room type

Moving to a dearer room does not silently change the price. If the guest is
paying for the upgrade, add the difference as a charge; if it is a goodwill
upgrade, add nothing and note the reason on the reservation.

<Tip>
Write the reason down: "moved from 204, air conditioner faulty". In three
weeks, when someone asks why a Standard guest slept in a Deluxe, the note is
the whole answer.
</Tip>

## Why the bill does not move

Charges belong to the **stay**, not the room. That is why a room move never
splits a bill in two, and why the guest's invoice at the end reads as one
continuous stay.
