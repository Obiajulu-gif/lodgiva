## Check a guest out

<Steps>

<Step>
Open **Front Desk → Today → Departures**, and find the guest.
</Step>

<Step>

### Review the bill

Open the folio and read it through: room nights, restaurant, laundry, VAT,
and any payments already taken. Fix mistakes now, not after the invoice is
issued.

</Step>

<Step>

### Take the balance

Record the payment with the tender used. Non-cash tenders need a reference:
the transfer narration, or the POS slip number.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check out

Select **Check out**. The reservation becomes `Checked Out`, and the room
becomes `Vacant` and `Dirty` for housekeeping.

</Step>

<Step>

### Issue the invoice

Issue the VAT invoice for the guest's records. It shows your TIN, VAT at
7.5% on its own line, and the total in naira.

</Step>

</Steps>

![A Lodgiva VAT invoice in naira](/images/docs/vat-invoice.jpg)

## If the guest still owes money

Lodgiva refuses the check-out and says so:

```text
Reservation RES-00042 still owes ₦96,750.00.
Settle the folio before checking the guest out.
```

This is deliberate: it is the control that stops a guest leaving unpaid
through a moment of inattention. Take the payment, or use the documented
exception below.

## Company and late-payment accounts

Where a company is genuinely invoiced later, a manager can allow the departure
without settlement. That permission is a setting on the property
(`allow_unsettled_departure`), not something the desk can decide alone, and
the balance stays on the books as owed.

<Warning>
Turning this on removes the guard for every departure at that property. Treat
it as a policy decision for accounts-based hotels, not a way around one
awkward morning.
</Warning>

## Late check-out

Charge it as its own line on the bill before checking out, so the invoice
explains itself.

## After check-out

The room appears on the housekeeping board as dirty. Once cleaned and
inspected it becomes sellable again. See [Housekeeping](/docs/housekeeping).
