---
title: "In-house guests"
description: "See who is in the hotel, what each guest owes, and post charges to a room."
---

The **In-house** list on the Front Desk screen shows every guest currently
staying, their room, their departure date and their balance.

## What the balance means

The balance is what the guest owes right now: charges posted, less payments
taken. Room charges for tonight post when the
[night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) runs, so a guest's balance grows
overnight, not at check-in.

## Post a charge to a room

Restaurant, bar, laundry and minibar all post to the guest's folio.

<Steps>

<Step>
Open the guest from the in-house list, then their bill.
</Step>

<Step>
Select **Add charge**, pick the charge type, describe it in words the guest
will recognise ("Dinner, table 4"), and enter the amount.
</Step>

<Step>
Save. The line appears on the bill immediately, with VAT applied as the
charge type dictates.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
Orders taken in the restaurant POS can be posted to a room directly, so the
desk does not re-key them.
</Note>

## Extending a stay

Open the reservation and change the departure date, or drag the bar on the
[tape chart](/docs/front-desk/tape-chart). Lodgiva checks the room is free for
the extra nights. If it is not, move the guest to a room that is.

## Watching balances

A large unpaid balance mid-stay is worth acting on before departure day.
Lodgiva will not let the guest check out owing money, so the awkward
conversation is better had early than at 7 a.m. with a taxi waiting.
