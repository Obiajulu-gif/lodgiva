---
title: "Group bookings"
description: "Hold blocks of rooms for a company, wedding or conference, and bill them together or separately."
---

A group booking holds several rooms under one name: a conference, a wedding
party, a company's staff.

## Create a group

<Steps>

<Step>
Go to **Front Desk → Groups → New group**.
</Step>

<Step>
Enter the group's name, the arrival and departure dates, and a **block**: how
many rooms of each type to hold.
</Step>

<Step>
Set a **cutoff date**: the date unsold rooms are released back for general
sale. Without one, held rooms sit empty until arrival.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Picking up rooms

As individual names come in, pick up a room from the block for each guest.
Each becomes an ordinary reservation, but linked to the group.

## Billing a group

Two arrangements, both supported:

- **Master bill.** Room charges go to one group folio the organiser settles.
  Guests pay their own extras.
- **Individual bills.** Each guest settles their own stay.

Charges can be moved between folios, so a guest's room night can be
transferred to the master bill while their bar tab stays with them. See
[Split bills](/docs/billing/split-bills).

<Tip>
Agree the split before arrival, in writing. Most group billing disputes are
really disagreements about what "the company is covering everything" meant.
</Tip>
