## Cancel a booking

<Steps>

<Step>
Open the reservation and select **Cancel**.
</Step>

<Step>

Lodgiva shows a preview first: what your
[cancellation policy](/docs/property/policies) charges for this booking on
this date, and what, if anything, is refundable.

</Step>

<Step>

Enter a reason. "Guest request", "Duplicate booking" and "Hotel unable to
accommodate" are different facts, and reports later depend on the difference.

</Step>

<Step>

Confirm. The reservation becomes `Cancelled`, the room is released for sale,
and any fee is posted to the folio.

</Step>

</Steps>

A cancellation letter can be produced for the guest, confirming what was
cancelled and what was charged.

## Refunding a deposit

If the deposit is refundable under your policy, refund it against the original
payment, recording how the money went back: cash from the drawer, or a
transfer with its reference. Refunds are recorded as their own lines, never by
deleting the original payment.

## No-shows

A guest who never arrives and never cancels is a no-show. The
[night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) flags these when it closes the day:
any reservation still `Confirmed` with an arrival date in the past becomes
`No Show`, and the policy fee is charged.

<Note>
This is one of the quiet reasons to run the audit every night. Rooms held for
guests who never came are only recorded as lost revenue if something marks
them so.
</Note>

## Shortening a stay

A guest leaving early is not a cancellation. Change the departure date on the
reservation, check whether your policy charges for the unused nights, and
check them out normally.
