## Check a guest in

<Steps>

<Step>
Open **Front Desk → Today**. Every guest due today is under **Arrivals**.
</Step>

<Step>
Find the guest and select **Check in**.
</Step>

<Step>

Choose a room, or let Lodgiva pick. It only offers rooms of the booked type
that are free and ready.

<Tip>
Rooms still marked `Dirty` are not offered. If housekeeping have finished but
not updated the board, ask them to mark the room ready; see
[Room status](/docs/housekeeping/room-status).
</Tip>

</Step>

<Step>

Confirm. The reservation becomes `Checked In`, the room becomes `Occupied`,
and the folio starts taking charges.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Registration and ID

Capture the guest's details on the profile: full name, phone, address, and an
ID if you take one. Nigerian installations offer **NIN** alongside passport
and driver's licence.

<Warning title="Handle ID copies carefully">
A photographed ID is personal data. Take it only if you need it, and see
[Guest privacy](/docs/guests/privacy) for how Lodgiva stores and removes it.
</Warning>

## Deposits at check-in

Take the deposit as a payment on the folio, choosing the tender used: cash,
bank transfer or POS terminal. Non-cash tenders require a reference.

## Early arrivals

If a guest arrives before check-in time and a clean room of their type is
free, check them in. If not, hold their luggage and check them in later.
Charging for early check-in is a charge type on the bill.

## Checking in a walk-in

Create the reservation first, then check in as above. See
[Take a booking](/docs/front-desk/create-a-booking#walk-ins).
