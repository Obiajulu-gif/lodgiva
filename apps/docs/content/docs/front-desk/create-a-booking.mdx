---

## Create the reservation

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the booking form

**Front Desk → New booking**, or press the New booking button on the tape
chart over the dates you want.

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter the dates and room type

Arrival and departure, number of adults and children, and the room type.
Lodgiva shows only what is genuinely free for those nights.

<Note>
A stay is counted in **nights**, not days. Arriving on the 10th and leaving on
the 12th is two nights.
</Note>

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter the guest

Name and phone number are the minimum. Start typing and Lodgiva searches your
existing guests, so regulars are recognised rather than duplicated.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the price

Lodgiva prices the stay from the room type and any
[season](/docs/property/rates) covering those nights, and shows VAT
separately:

```text
2 nights × ₦45,000     ₦90,000
VAT 7.5%                ₦6,750
Total                  ₦96,750
```

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

The reservation is created. Depending on your settings it is `Confirmed`
straight away, or `Pending Payment` until a deposit lands. See
[Reservation statuses](/docs/getting-started/core-concepts#reservation).

</Step>

</Steps>

## Walk-ins

A guest at the desk with no booking is handled the same way: create the
reservation with today's date, then check them in immediately. The only
difference is that you assign the room yourself.

## Taking a deposit

Record it on the bill as a payment as soon as it arrives. Bank transfers need
the narration or reference, so the money can be matched later. See
[Payments](/docs/billing/payments).

## When everything is full

Lodgiva refuses to oversell. If nothing is free, add the enquiry to the
**waitlist**: if a booking cancels, the waitlisted guest appears as ready to
contact.

## Direct bookings from your page

Bookings made by guests themselves at `/book` arrive as ordinary reservations
and appear in Arrivals like any other. See
[Your booking page](/docs/property/booking-page).
