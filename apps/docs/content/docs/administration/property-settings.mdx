Found under **Settings → Property**.

## Identity

Name, address, city, state, **country**, phone, email and TIN. These appear on
invoices and on your booking page.

## Times and dates

- **Check-in time** and **check-out time**, shown to guests and used for early
  and late charges.
- The **business date**, which moves with the
  [night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) and should never be edited by hand.

## Tax

Currency, tax rate and how tax is labelled. Nigerian properties get naira and
7.5% VAT; see [Nigerian settings](/docs/administration/nigeria).

## Guardrails

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Overbooking allowance** | How far past your room count you may sell. Zero means never |
| **No-show charge** | What a no-show is charged: nothing, the first night, or the full stay |
| **Allow unsettled departure** | Lets a guest check out owing money |

<Danger title="Allow unsettled departure">
This turns off the guard that stops unpaid check-outs — for **every** departure
at the property, not one. Turn it on only if your hotel genuinely invoices
companies after the stay, and say so in writing to the desk. Never turn it on
to get past one difficult morning.
</Danger>

## Cancellation policy

How late a guest may cancel free of charge, and what is charged after that.
See [Policies](/docs/property/policies).

## Booking page

Cover photo, room-type photos and descriptions. See
[Your booking page](/docs/property/booking-page).
