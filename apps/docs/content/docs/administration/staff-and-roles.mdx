title: "Staff and roles"
description: "Create accounts for your team and give each person the permissions their job needs, and no more."
---

Every member of staff gets their own account. Shared logins make it impossible
to answer the only question that matters after money goes missing: who did
this?

## The roles

| Role | For | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Front Desk** | Receptionists | Bookings, check-in and check-out, charges, payments |
| **Housekeeping** | Floor staff and supervisors | Room status, tasks, the phone screen |
| **Finance** | Cashiers, accounts | Invoices, refunds, cashier sessions, financial reports |
| **Revenue Manager** | Whoever sets prices | Rates, seasons, availability, revenue reports |
| **Hotel Admin** | Manager or owner | Property settings, staff, everything operational |
| **Kamra Agent** | Automations and integrations | The same operational surface as Front Desk, for software |
| **System Manager** | Technical administrator | The underlying platform |

<Note title="Why a role is called Kamra Agent">
Lodgiva is built on the open-source Kamra PMS, and the automation role keeps
its upstream name. Renaming it would break every integration built against it
upstream, for no gain. It is the role used by software acting on the hotel's
behalf, not a person.
</Note>

## Add a member of staff

<Steps>

<Step>
Go to **Settings → Users → New**.
</Step>

<Step>
Enter their name and email address. The email is their username.
</Step>

<Step>
Give them the role or roles their job needs. Most receptionists need only
**Front Desk**.
</Step>

<Step>
Save. They receive an invitation to set their own password.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Give the least access that works

Ask what the person must do, not what they might one day do. A receptionist
who cannot cancel an invoice cannot be persuaded, pressured or tricked into
cancelling one.

Add permissions when someone is blocked. That is one awkward minute, against a
standing risk.

## When someone leaves

Disable the account the day they leave. Do not delete it: their work stays
attached to it, and the history is what makes past shifts auditable.

<Warning>
Disable, never share. An account passed on to the next person destroys every
record of who did what before that point.
</Warning>
