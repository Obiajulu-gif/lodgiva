Lodgiva is organised around the questions a desk actually asks during a shift.

## Today

The opening screen. It answers "what is happening now?":

- **Arrivals** expected today, with a Check in button on each.
- **Departures** due out.
- **In-house** guests, their rooms, and what each owes.
- **Occupancy** and last night's revenue.
- The **room board**, colour-coded by housekeeping status.

![The Front Desk overview: arrivals, departures, in-house guests, occupancy, revenue and the room board](/images/docs/front-desk.jpg)

## Tape chart

Rooms down the side, nights across the top, bookings as bars. It answers
"where is everyone, and what is free?". Drag a booking to move a guest or
change dates.

![The tape chart: rooms down the side, nights across the top, each booking a bar](/images/docs/tape-chart.jpg)

See [The tape chart](/docs/front-desk/tape-chart).

## Reservations

Every booking, searchable by guest name, phone or confirmation number. Open
one to see the stay, the bill and the guest's history in a single panel.

## Guests

Guest profiles with past stays, preferences and lifetime value. See
[Guests](/docs/guests).

## Billing

Bills, payments and invoices, plus the cashier drawer. See
[Billing](/docs/billing).

![A VAT invoice in naira: 7.5% VAT on its own line, the total spelled out in naira and kobo](/images/docs/vat-invoice.jpg)

## Housekeeping

The room status board and today's tasks, with a phone-sized version for staff
on the floor at `/hk`. See [Housekeeping](/docs/housekeeping).

## Where each role lands

| Role | Uses most |
| --- | --- |
| Front Desk | Today, Reservations, Billing |
| Housekeeping | Room board, phone app |
| Finance | Billing, cashier sessions, reports |
| Hotel Admin | Settings, staff, rates |
