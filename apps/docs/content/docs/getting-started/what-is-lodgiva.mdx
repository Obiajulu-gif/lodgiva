title: "What is Lodgiva?"
description: "Lodgiva is hotel management software for Nigerian hotels: front desk, guest bills, housekeeping and the nightly close, with naira and VAT built in."
---

Lodgiva runs the daily operation of a hotel. One system holds the bookings,
the rooms, the guests and the money, so the front desk, housekeeping and the
owner all work from the same information.

## What it does today

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Front desk** | See today's arrivals and departures, take bookings, check guests in and out, move rooms, read the tape chart |
| **Rooms and rates** | Room types, rooms, prices, seasonal pricing, overbooking limits |
| **Guest bills** | Charges, payments, VAT invoices in naira, split bills, credit notes |
| **Payments** | Cash, bank transfer and POS terminal, each recorded with a reference |
| **Cash control** | Cashier drawers that must balance before they close |
| **Nightly close** | Night audit: posts room charges, flags no-shows, moves the business date on |
| **Housekeeping** | Room status board, tasks, and a phone-sized screen for the floor |
| **Direct bookings** | Your own booking page, with live prices in naira and no commission |
| **Restaurant and bar** | POS with orders posted to a room bill or settled at the till |

## Who it is for

- **Hotel owners** want to know last night's revenue, and that nobody left
  without paying. See [Reports](/docs/billing/reports) and
  [Night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit).
- **Managers** set prices and room types, approve refunds and cash variances,
  and add staff. See [Rooms and rates](/docs/property) and
  [Administration](/docs/administration).
- **Front-desk staff** spend the shift on arrivals, departures and bills. See
  [Front desk](/docs/front-desk).
- **Housekeeping** work from the room board on a phone. See
  [Housekeeping](/docs/housekeeping).
- **Developers and partners** integrate with the API. See
  [Developers](/docs/developers).

## What makes it Nigerian

Most hotel software assumes another country's tax and payment habits. Lodgiva
ships a Nigerian layer:

- Prices, bills and reports in **naira (₦)**, stored to the kobo.
- **VAT at 7.5%** on its own invoice line, and a **TIN** on the invoice. The
  rate is a configurable default, not tax advice.
- **Bank transfer** and **POS terminal** as first-class payment methods, each
  needing a reference, because that is how Nigerian hotels are actually paid.
- **Africa/Lagos** time, Nigerian guest nationality, and **NIN** as an ID type.

See [Nigerian settings](/docs/administration/nigeria) for the detail.
