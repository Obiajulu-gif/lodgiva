---
title: "Create your hotel"
description: "Use the setup wizard to create your property, room types, rooms and rates, and understand why the country field matters."
---

A fresh Lodgiva installation has no hotel in it. The setup wizard at
**`/lodgiva/setup`** creates everything at once: the property, its room types,
its rooms and its meal plans.

## What you need first

- The hotel's legal and trading name, and its address.
- Your room categories and the nightly price of each.
- The room numbers in each category.
- Your TIN, if you issue VAT invoices.

## Fill in the wizard

<Steps>

<Step>

### The property

Name, city and state. **Set the country to Nigeria.**

<Danger title="The country field decides your tax">
Lodgiva picks its tax rules from the property's country. Set to Nigeria, you
get naira, 7.5% VAT on its own line and a TIN field. Left blank, the
underlying platform falls back to Indian tax rules, and your invoices will be
wrong. See [Nigerian settings](/docs/administration/nigeria).
</Danger>

</Step>

<Step>

### Room types

One row per category you sell, for example:

| Code | Name | Price per night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `STD` | Standard | ₦45,000 |
| `DLX` | Deluxe | ₦85,000 |
| `EXS` | Executive Suite | ₦150,000 |

Prices are what the guest pays before VAT. Lodgiva adds VAT as its own line
on the bill.

Leave the tax percent empty unless a room type is taxed differently. Empty
means "use the property's standard rate", which is what you want.

</Step>

<Step>

### Rooms

List the room numbers in each type, such as `101, 102, 103` for Deluxe. You
can add floors later.

<Tip>
Use the numbers on the doors. Staff search by them all day.
</Tip>

</Step>

<Step>

### Finish

Lodgiva creates everything and opens the Front Desk. You can change any of it
afterwards under **Settings**.

</Step>

</Steps>
