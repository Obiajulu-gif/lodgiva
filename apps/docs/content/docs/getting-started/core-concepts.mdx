---
title: "Core concepts"
description: "The seven ideas the rest of Lodgiva is built on: property, room type, room, rate, reservation, folio and business date."
---

Learn these seven words and every other page makes sense.

## Property

One hotel. It holds the address, the currency, the time zone, check-in and
check-out times, and the tax settings. Lodgiva can run more than one property
on the same installation.

## Room type

A category of room you sell, such as Standard, Deluxe or Executive Suite. The
room type carries the **price**, how many adults it sleeps, and its photos.
Guests book a room type, not a specific room.

## Room

A physical room with a number, such as 101. Each room belongs to one room
type, sits on a floor, and has a housekeeping status.

<Note>
Rooms and room types are separate for a reason: a guest books "a Deluxe", and
the front desk decides which Deluxe. That is what lets you move a guest
without touching their booking.
</Note>

## Rate

What a room type costs per night. The room type has a base price, and you can
vary it by season or day of week. See [Prices and seasons](/docs/property/rates).

## Reservation

One guest's stay: who is coming, which room type, arrival and departure dates,
and its status. A reservation moves through these states:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Inquiry`, `Quoted`, `Requested` | Being discussed; holds no room |
| `Held` | Room held briefly while the guest decides |
| `Pending Payment` | Awaiting a deposit |
| `Confirmed` | On the books, room reserved |
| `Checked In` | Guest is in the hotel |
| `Checked Out` | Stay finished |
| `Cancelled` | Called off before arrival |
| `No Show` | Never arrived; flagged by the night audit |
| `Waitlist` | No room available, kept in case one frees up |

## Folio

The guest's bill. Every room night, restaurant order and payment lands on the
folio as a separate line. A folio is never edited: a mistake is corrected with
a **reversal** line, so the history stays intact.

One stay can have more than one folio, which is how you split a bill between a
company and a guest.

## Business date

The hotel's own working day, which is **not** the clock date. It moves forward
only when the [night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) runs. This is why a
3 a.m. arrival still belongs to the previous night's business.

<Tip>
If room charges look missing, check the business date before anything else.
The night audit posts the night's room charges as it closes the day.
</Tip>

## How they fit together

```text
Property
 └── Room type  (Deluxe, ₦85,000)
      └── Room  (101, 102, 103)

Guest ──► Reservation ──► assigned a Room
                   │
                   └────► Folio ──► charges + payments ──► Invoice
```
