This page takes an empty Lodgiva installation to a guest checked into a room.

<Steps>

<Step>

## Sign in

Open your Lodgiva address and add `/lodgiva`, for example
`https://hotel.example.com/lodgiva`.

Sign in as **`Administrator`** with the password from your installation. If
someone else installed Lodgiva, ask them for it.

<Note>
`Administrator` is a username, not an email address. Once you are in, add
real staff accounts with their own names; see
[Staff and roles](/docs/administration/staff-and-roles).
</Note>

</Step>

<Step>

## Create your hotel

If no property exists, Lodgiva opens the setup wizard at `/lodgiva/setup`.
Fill in:

- the hotel's name and city;
- **country: Nigeria** — this one field decides the tax and currency;
- your room types, with a nightly price;
- the room numbers for each type.

Full detail: [Create your hotel](/docs/getting-started/create-your-hotel).

</Step>

<Step>

## Take a booking

From **Front Desk → New booking**, enter the guest's name and phone number,
the arrival and departure dates, and the room type. Lodgiva prices the stay,
including VAT, and reserves a room.

See [Take a booking](/docs/front-desk/create-a-booking).

</Step>

<Step>

## Check the guest in

On the day of arrival the booking appears under **Arrivals** on the Front Desk
screen. Select **Check in**. Lodgiva assigns a room if one is not already
chosen, and marks it occupied.

See [Check in](/docs/front-desk/check-in).

</Step>

<Step>

## Take payment and check out

Open the guest's bill, record the payment, then check them out.

Cash, bank transfer and POS terminal are all supported. Non-cash payments need
a reference: the bank narration or the terminal slip number.

<Warning>
Lodgiva refuses to check out a guest who still owes money, and refuses to
issue an invoice for an unpaid bill. That is deliberate. See
[Payments](/docs/billing/payments).
</Warning>

</Step>

</Steps>
