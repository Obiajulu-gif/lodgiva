When a stay is settled, Lodgiva closes the bill and issues an invoice.

![A Lodgiva VAT invoice: naira totals, VAT on its own line, TIN in the header](/images/docs/vat-invoice.jpg)

## What the invoice shows

- Your hotel's name, address and **TIN**.
- The guest, or the company being billed.
- Every charge, with dates.
- **VAT at 7.5% on its own line**, not folded into the prices.
- The total in naira, and the amount in words, to the kobo.
- The invoice number and the date it was issued.

## Issue an invoice

<Steps>

<Step>
Settle the bill: the balance must be zero.
</Step>

<Step>
Select **Close folio and issue invoice**. Depending on your settings, a
cashier PIN is required.
</Step>

<Step>
Print it, or send the PDF to the guest.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Warning>
An unpaid bill cannot be invoiced. An invoice asserts that the stay was paid
for; issuing one against an unpaid folio would put a false statement into your
records and your VAT return.
</Warning>

## Company invoices

Put the company's name, address and TIN on the guest profile before issuing,
so they appear on the invoice. Companies routinely reject invoices missing
their TIN.

## Cancelling an invoice

An invoice already issued is cancelled with a reason, not deleted. The
cancellation is recorded, the number is not reused, and the original remains
visible. This is the only honest way to correct an invoice after the fact.

## Finding an invoice again

Search by invoice number, guest name or date under **Billing → Invoices**.

## Nigerian e-invoicing

<Status type="planned" /> Direct submission to FIRS is not implemented.
Invoices carry the information a Nigerian VAT invoice requires, and can be
exported for filing.
