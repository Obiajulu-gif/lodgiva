---
title: "Split bills"
description: "Let a company pay the room while the guest pays their own extras."
---

One stay can carry more than one bill. The common case: a company pays the
room and breakfast, the guest pays the bar.

## Split a bill

<Steps>

<Step>
Open the reservation and select **Split folio**. A second folio is created for
the same stay.
</Step>

<Step>
Move the lines that belong to the second bill onto it. Charges can be moved
one at a time or several at once.
</Step>

<Step>
Settle and invoice each bill separately. The company gets its invoice, the
guest gets theirs.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Splitting a single charge

A charge can also be split between two bills by amount: half a ₦40,000 line to
the company, half to the guest. Both halves keep their link to the original.

## Group masters

For a [group booking](/docs/front-desk/group-bookings), room charges can be
routed to one master folio that the organiser settles, while each guest keeps
their own bill for extras.

<Tip>
Agree the split before arrival and record it on the reservation. Most group
disputes are arguments about what "the company covers everything" was meant to
include.
</Tip>
