---
title: "Refunds and corrections"
description: "Fix a wrong charge, refund a payment, and return a deposit, without deleting anything."
---

Mistakes happen. Lodgiva's rule is that they are **corrected**, never erased.

## A charge posted in error

Void it. The original line stays, and a reversing line is posted against it
with your reason. The bill then shows both, which is exactly what an auditor
needs to see.

You will be asked for a reason. Write a real one: "posted to the wrong room",
not "error".

## Refunding a payment

Refund against the original payment, choosing how the money went back — cash
from the drawer, or a transfer with its reference. The original payment is
untouched; the refund is its own line.

<Warning>
Never "fix" an over-payment by deleting the payment line. The money genuinely
arrived; a deleted line means your drawer or your bank statement will not
balance at the end of the day.
</Warning>

## Security deposits

A refundable deposit is returned when the guest leaves, once the room has been
checked. Lodgiva lists deposits awaiting refund so none is quietly kept.

## Cancelling an invoice

See [VAT invoices](/docs/billing/invoices#cancelling-an-invoice). Cancellation
requires a reason and, depending on your settings, a cashier PIN.

## Who may do what

Voids, refunds and invoice cancellations are the operations most open to
abuse, so they are restricted by role and recorded against the person who did
them. See [Staff and roles](/docs/administration/staff-and-roles).
