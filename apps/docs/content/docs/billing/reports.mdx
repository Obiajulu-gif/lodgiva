## The manager's flash

One screen for the owner or manager: last night's occupancy, rooms sold,
revenue, and how the month is running against budget.

## The numbers that matter

| Figure | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Occupancy** | Rooms sold ÷ rooms available, as a percentage |
| **ADR** | Average daily rate: room revenue ÷ rooms sold |
| **RevPAR** | Revenue per available room: room revenue ÷ rooms available |

RevPAR is the one to watch. It captures both how full you are and what you
sold for, which is why a hotel can be busier and worse off at the same time.

## Other reports

- **Budget against actual**, by month, once targets are set.
- **Contribution**, showing where revenue came from.
- **Voids and allowances**, showing what was reversed or given away, and by
  whom.
- **Service levels**, for housekeeping and maintenance response.

## Where the figures come from

Every figure is derived from the folios and reservations, on the **business
date**, not the clock date. A report for "yesterday" means the business day
the [night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) closed.

<Note>
If a day looks empty, the audit for it probably has not run. The report is
telling the truth about a day that was never closed.
</Note>
