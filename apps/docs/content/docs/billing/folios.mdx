---
title: "Bills (folios)"
description: "How a guest's bill is built, what it holds, and why lines are reversed rather than deleted."
---

A **folio** is a guest's bill for one stay. Everything that happens to their
money lands on it as a line: room nights, restaurant, laundry, VAT, payments
and refunds.

## The shape of a bill

```text
Folio F-00123 · Chinedu Okafor · Room 101 · 10–12 Sep

Charges
  10 Sep  Room night, Deluxe            ₦85,000.00
  10 Sep  VAT 7.5%                       ₦6,375.00
  11 Sep  Dinner, table 4                ₦12,500.00
  11 Sep  Room night, Deluxe            ₦85,000.00
  11 Sep  VAT 7.5%                       ₦6,375.00

Payments
  10 Sep  Bank Transfer  REF 8832019     ₦100,000.00

Balance due                              ₦95,250.00
```

## Why a bill is never edited

A folio line, once posted, is never changed or removed. A mistake is corrected
by posting a **reversal**: a second line that cancels the first, with a reason.

This is what makes the bill trustworthy. Anyone reading it later — an owner, an
auditor, the guest — sees what happened, including the correction. A bill you
can quietly edit is a bill that proves nothing.

## Opening and closing

A folio opens when the guest checks in, or when the
[night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) finds an in-house guest without one.

It **closes** when the stay is settled and the invoice is issued. A closed
folio is final. If something is genuinely wrong afterwards, the invoice is
cancelled with a reason, which leaves a record, rather than being erased.

<Warning>
Lodgiva refuses to close a folio that still has a balance. If you meet that
message, take the payment; do not look for a way around it.
</Warning>

## More than one bill per stay

A stay can carry several folios: a Guest folio and an Extra folio, or a
company master. See [Split bills](/docs/billing/split-bills).
