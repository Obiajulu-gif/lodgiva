## The tenders

Lodgiva's Nigerian setup offers:

| Tender | Reference needed | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash** | No | Goes into the cashier drawer |
| **Bank Transfer** | **Yes** | The narration or transaction reference |
| **POS Terminal** | **Yes** | The terminal slip number |
| **Card** | **Yes** | Authorisation or slip number |
| **Company Credit** | Yes | The company's reference or PO |
| **OTA Prepaid** | Yes | Already collected by the channel |
| **Payment Link** | Yes | Sent to the guest to pay |

## Record a payment

<Steps>

<Step>
Open the guest's bill and select **Add payment**.
</Step>

<Step>
Choose the tender, enter the amount in naira, and enter the reference for
anything that is not cash.
</Step>

<Step>
Save. The balance drops immediately, and cash payments go to the open
[cashier session](/docs/billing/cashier).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Why references are required

Lodgiva refuses a non-cash payment without a reference:

```text
Bank Transfer payments need a reference (the bank narration or
transaction ID). Cash is the only tender that may be recorded
without one.
```

And it refuses the **same reference twice** on one bill:

```text
Reference 8832019 has already been recorded on this folio.
If this is a second, separate payment, use its own reference.
```

Both guard the same failure: a transfer recorded twice, or recorded with
nothing to match it against at the end of the month. A reference you cannot
find in the bank statement is money you cannot prove arrived.

<Tip>
Enter the reference while the guest is standing there. Reconstructing it a
week later from a screenshot on a phone is how hotels lose afternoons.
</Tip>

## Part payments

A guest may pay in instalments: a deposit on booking, the balance at
departure. Each is its own payment line with its own reference.

## Overpayment

If a guest pays more than they owe, the balance goes negative and is refunded
at departure. See [Refunds](/docs/billing/refunds).

## Card payments

<Status type="planned" /> Direct Paystack and Flutterwave integration is not
