## The charge types

| Type | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `Room` | The nightly room charge, posted by the night audit |
| `Meal Plan` | Breakfast or board included in a rate plan |
| `Food & Beverage` | Restaurant and bar |
| `Minibar` | In-room consumption |
| `Laundry` | Guest laundry |
| `Spa` | Spa and treatments |
| `Early Check-in` | Arriving before check-in time |
| `Late Checkout` | Leaving after check-out time |
| `Cleaning Fee` | Extra cleaning, damage-free |
| `Discount` | A reduction agreed at the time |
| `Allowance` | A reduction given afterwards, usually to fix a problem |
| `Misc` | Anything that fits nowhere else |

<Tip>
Use `Misc` sparingly. A month of `Misc` lines is a month you cannot analyse.
</Tip>

## Post a charge

From the guest's bill, select **Add charge**, choose the type, write a
description the guest will recognise, and enter the amount before VAT.

## VAT

VAT is calculated from the charge type and the property's tax settings, and
posted as **its own line**, not folded into the price. That is what lets the
invoice show the tax separately, as a VAT invoice must.

The Nigerian default is **7.5%**. See [Nigerian settings](/docs/administration/nigeria).

## Discounts and allowances

Both reduce what the guest pays, but they mean different things:

- A **discount** is agreed before or at the time: a corporate rate, a long-stay
  reduction.
- An **allowance** is given afterwards to put something right: the air
  conditioner failed, so a night is reduced.

Keeping them apart lets a manager see how much revenue is being given away to
fix problems, which is a different number from what is being negotiated away.

<Note>
Allowances are reported on their own, alongside voided charges, so that
patterns are visible. Repeated allowances on the same room usually mean the
room, not the guest, is the problem.
</Note>

## Posting from the restaurant

Orders taken in the POS can be sent straight to a guest's room bill, so the
desk does not re-key them and the guest signs once.
