## "Wrong email, username, or password"

Usually the account does not exist rather than the password being wrong. The
first account is **`Administrator`**, a username, not an email address. Staff
accounts must be created before they can sign in; see
[Staff and roles](/docs/administration/staff-and-roles).

## Room charges are missing

The [night audit](/docs/billing/night-audit) posts room charges. Check:

1. Has the audit run for that date? Look at the Night Audit Run records.
2. Is the business date what you expect?
3. Is Frappe's scheduler running? Without it, nothing fires at 3 a.m.

## A room will not appear at check-in

Check-in offers only rooms that are free **and** ready. Check the room's
housekeeping status: `Dirty` and `Out of Order` rooms are not offered. See
[Room status](/docs/housekeeping/room-status).

## "Sold out" when rooms look free

Lodgiva counts a room as sold for the whole night. A room occupied by a guest
leaving tomorrow is not available tonight. If the overbooking allowance is
zero — the default and the right setting — nothing may be sold beyond the room
count.

## A guest cannot be checked out

They owe money. Take the payment. If your hotel genuinely invoices companies
after the stay, see the departure setting in
[Property settings](/docs/administration/property-settings), and understand
that it applies to every departure.

## A payment will not save

Non-cash tenders need a reference, and the same reference cannot be used twice
on one bill. Both messages say exactly which rule was hit. See
[Payments](/docs/billing/payments).

## The site will not load

<Steps>

<Step>
Can you reach the server at all? `ping` it, then `ssh` to it.
</Step>

<Step>
Are the services running? On the server, check the Frappe and proxy services.
</Step>

<Step>
Is the disk full? A full disk stops MariaDB, which looks like everything
breaking at once.
</Step>

<Step>
Has the certificate expired? The installer renews automatically, but a server
that was off for weeks may need a moment to catch up.
</Step>

</Steps>

## After an update something broke

Restore the backup you took before updating, and report the problem with the
version you moved from and to. See [Contributing](/docs/resources/contributing).

## Still stuck

Open an issue with: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the
exact message. A screenshot of the message is worth a paragraph describing it.
