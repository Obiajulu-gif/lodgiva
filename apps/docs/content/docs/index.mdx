Lodgiva is hotel management software for Nigerian hotels, serviced apartments
and small hotel groups. It covers the work a property does every day: taking
bookings, checking guests in and out, putting charges on a bill, taking
payment, and closing the day's business.

It is built on [Frappe](https://frappe.io) and the open-source
[Kamra PMS](https://github.com/Kamra-PMS/kamra-pms), with a Nigerian layer of
its own: naira, 7.5% VAT on its own line, TIN on invoices, bank transfer and
POS terminal as first-class payment methods, and controls that stop money
going missing.

## What this documentation covers

These pages document the **Lodgiva PMS**: the system hotel staff use, built on
Frappe. Everything described here was checked against the running product.

Lodgiva is made of three repositories:

| Repository | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [`lodgiva-pms`](https://github.com/Obiajulu-gif/lodgiva-pms) | The PMS itself: front desk, bookings, billing, housekeeping, POS |
| [`lodgiva-nigeria`](https://github.com/Obiajulu-gif/lodgiva-nigeria) | Naira, VAT and TIN, Nigerian defaults, the money controls, branding |
| [`lodgiva`](https://github.com/Obiajulu-gif/lodgiva) | The public website, these documents, and the installer |
