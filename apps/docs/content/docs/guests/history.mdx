Open a guest and Lodgiva shows their journey: every stay, what it earned, and
what has happened along the way.

![A guest profile with stays, spend and history](/images/docs/guest-journey.jpg)

## What it shows

- Every reservation, with dates, room and status.
- What each stay was worth, and their total to date.
- Cancellations and no-shows, which are part of the picture too.
- Notes staff have added.

## Using it well

- **At check-in**, a quick look tells you this is their fourth stay. Saying so
  costs nothing and is remembered.
- **When quoting**, a guest who has stayed eight times is worth a held rate
  more than a discount to someone who will never return.
- **When something went wrong** last time, the note is there before the guest
  has to repeat it.

<Tip>
Write preferences down, not personal opinions. "Prefers a high floor, away
from the lift" is useful to everyone. Anything you would not want the guest to
read should not be in the system at all.
</Tip>
