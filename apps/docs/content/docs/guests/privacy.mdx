A hotel holds real personal data: names, phone numbers, addresses, ID
documents and payment references. Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 applies
to it.

<Note>
This page describes what the software does. It is not legal advice; your
obligations depend on your hotel and how you operate.
</Note>

## What is held

| Data | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, phone, email, address | Guest profile | Booking and invoicing |
| ID type and number | Guest profile | Registration, where required |
| ID photograph | Attached to the guest | Registration |
| Stay history | Reservations | Operations and reporting |
| Payment references | Folio payments | Reconciling money received |

Lodgiva never stores full card numbers. Card and POS payments are recorded by
their **reference**, such as the terminal slip number, not the card details.

## ID documents

Photographed IDs are stored as private files, reachable only by signed-in
staff with permission. Take them only where you need them, and do not keep
them longer than you must.

## Removing a guest's data

Lodgiva can **anonymise** a guest: the personal details are cleared while the
financial history stays intact, because invoices must remain auditable even
when the person behind them asks to be forgotten.

<Warning>
Anonymising cannot be reversed. Confirm the request and note who approved it
before running it.
</Warning>

## Practical rules for staff

- Do not photograph an ID "just in case".
- Do not send guest lists over WhatsApp.
- Give each member of staff their own account, so access is traceable. See
  [Staff and roles](/docs/administration/staff-and-roles).
