Every room carries a housekeeping status and an occupancy status, and they are
independent: an occupied room can be clean or dirty.

## The statuses

| Status | Meaning | Sellable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Dirty` | Needs cleaning, usually after a departure | No |
| `Clean` | Cleaned, not yet inspected | Depends on your policy |
| `Inspected` | Checked by a supervisor | Yes |
| `Ready` | Ready to sell | Yes |
| `Out of Order` | Cannot be used: maintenance | No |

Occupancy — `Vacant` or `Occupied` — is set by Lodgiva from check-ins and
check-outs. Nobody sets it by hand.

## The board

The room board shows every room, colour-coded. At a glance: what is dirty,
what is ready, what is occupied, what is out of order.

## How a room moves round the cycle

```text
Guest checks out  →  Dirty
Cleaned           →  Clean
Inspected         →  Inspected / Ready
Guest checks in   →  Occupied
```

## Why it matters to the desk

Check-in only offers rooms that are free **and** ready. A room cleaned an hour
ago but never marked clean is a room you cannot sell, and a guest standing in
reception while a supervisor is telephoned.

<Tip>
Have floor staff update the status as they leave each room, on their phone.
The board is only as useful as it is current.
</Tip>
