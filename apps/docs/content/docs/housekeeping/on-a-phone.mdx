---
title: "Housekeeping on a phone"
description: "The phone-sized screen floor staff use to see their rooms and update status."
---

Housekeeping staff do not sit at a desk. Lodgiva has a phone-sized screen for
them at **`/hk`**, for example `https://hotel.example.com/hk`.

## What it shows

- The rooms assigned to them today.
- Each room's status and what it needs.
- Buttons big enough to press with one thumb, holding a pile of towels.

## What they can do

- Claim and accept a task.
- Mark a room clean, or ready.
- Attach a photograph.
- Report a problem, which raises it for maintenance.

## Setting staff up

<Steps>

<Step>
Create an account for each housekeeper with the **Housekeeping** role. See
[Staff and roles](/docs/administration/staff-and-roles).
</Step>

<Step>
Open `/hk` on their phone, sign in, and add the page to the home screen so it
opens like an app.
</Step>

<Step>
Show them the two buttons they actually need. Everything else can wait.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
Hotel Wi-Fi rarely reaches every corridor. A room updated in the lift, on
mobile data, still beats a paper list handed in at four o'clock.
</Tip>
