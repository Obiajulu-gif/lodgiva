---
title: "Maintenance and out-of-order rooms"
description: "Take a room out of service for repairs and put it back when the work is done."
---

## Taking a room out of service

Set the room's housekeeping status to **Out of Order**. Immediately:

- it stops being offered at check-in;
- it disappears from your booking page;
- the tape chart marks it as unsellable.

Record **why** and, if you can, when it is expected back.

## Putting it back

Set it back to clean or ready once the work is done and the room has been
checked. Nothing else brings it back automatically.

<Warning>
A room left Out of Order after the repair is finished is silent lost revenue.
Check the out-of-order list weekly; it is the sort of thing that goes unnoticed
for a month.
</Warning>

## Repeated faults

Problems reported against a room build a history. When the same room keeps
generating allowances and complaints, that is the case for spending money on
it, with the evidence attached.
