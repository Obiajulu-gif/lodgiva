---
title: "Housekeeping tasks"
description: "Assign cleaning and maintenance work, track who accepted it, and see how long it took."
---

A task is a piece of work on a room: clean after departure, a deep clean, a
maintenance check.

## The life of a task

<Steps>

<Step>
**Created** — by a supervisor, or automatically when a guest checks out.
</Step>

<Step>
**Assigned or claimed** — a supervisor assigns it, or a member of staff claims
it from the list.
</Step>

<Step>
**Accepted or rejected** — the person doing it accepts. A rejection takes a
reason, so the work goes back to the list with an explanation rather than
sitting unnoticed.
</Step>

<Step>
**Done** — marked complete, with a photograph if your hotel requires one.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Photographs

Staff can attach photographs to a task: a stain on a carpet, a broken kettle,
or simply the finished room. It settles most arguments before they start.

## Response times

How long tasks take from creation to completion is reported, so a floor that
is consistently slow at turning rooms round becomes visible before it costs
you an arrival.

<Note>
Use the numbers to find bottlenecks, not to rank people. A housekeeper with
slow times is often the one given the hardest rooms.
</Note>
